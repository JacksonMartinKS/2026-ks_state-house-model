How the forecast works

Each contested district starts from a baseline partisan lean (recent presidential results blended with down-ballot statewide races), then adds a statewide generic-ballot shift, an incumbency effect, a candidate-quality rating built from each candidate's past over- or underperformance, and a campaign-finance effect. The simulated elections share statewide errors, so districts rise and fall together.
